Most AI demos stop at chat.

I built **ACL (Agent Contract Language)** to help AI take real actions in apps **safely** — with a receipt for every run.

Think: APIs give access. ACL gives control.

Live demos:
- https://acl.fyi/agenticflow

## Reply 1
What ACL is (plain English):

ACL is an OSS language/runtime that sits between:
- app capabilities (APIs, MCP, Zapier, CLI)
- an AI interface (chat/voice)
- and a safety model (checks, rules, receipts)
